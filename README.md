<p style="text-align: center;">Clockwork adds a range of unique mechanical trinkets and tools based on the style of technology from the Arcane series!<br>This project is currently in <strong>BETA</strong>, and most features are unfinished or unobtainable in survival. However, the playable release is planned to be wrapped up by the 22nd!<br><strong>Fabric port &amp; 1.21.1 port are both planned.</strong><br><br>Content:</p>
<p style="text-align: center;"><strong>Clockwork dragonfly:</strong> Currently, the dragonfly has no spawn egg, and has to be summoned with commands. When summoned, the dragonfly can be ridden as a flying &amp; walking mount.<br><strong>Clockwork crossbows:</strong> The crossbow has 2 variants - Scoped, and automatic. The scoped crossbow shoots arrows which travel faster and further, and is equipped with a spyglass for increased accuracy. The automatic crossbow comes with a barrel, which automatically reloads arrows at twice the speed, allowing for rapid fire!<br><strong>Potion sprayer:</strong> This item uses potions as ammo, just aim and right click to shoot a mist of potion droplets, only using a fraction of the potions duration!<br><strong>Clockwork arrow:</strong> A simple seeking arrow that tilts towards the closest mob to your crosshair when fired.<br><strong>Clockwork wings:</strong> An elytra variant with less gliding power, but a flap boost ability!<br>&nbsp;</p>
